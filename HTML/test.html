Operation Briefing

Mission

Force Chinese amphibious landing operations to fail.

Intelligence

  • China is expected to launch a full-scale missile attack at 0240.
  • China has deployed numerous batteries of multiple launch rocket systems on Pingtan Island and cruise missiles near Quanzhou Estuarine.
  • China is expected to choose Penghu, Taoyuan International Airport and Zhuwei Beach as their landing locations.

Objectives

  • Protect underground shelters at Jiashan Airbase.
  • Disperse some air assets from airbases to contingency runways to spread loss.
  • Conserve SAM and anti-ship missile batteries as much as possible to counter follow-on Chinese amphibious landing.
  • Break the kill chain of the Chinese ballistic missile strike by destroying UAVs such as WZ-8s or BZK-005s.
  • Find and neutralize units on Pingtan Island and near Quanzhou Estuarine.
  • Disable the IADS along the Chinese coast and sink the Chinese CSG entering Bachi Channel in order to break the Chinese blockade of Taiwan.
  • Prevent Chinese forces from establishing beachheads at Zhuwei Beach and Penghu and capturing the airfield at the Taoyuan International Airport.

Rules of Engagement (ROE)

You are not allowed to attack first before the Chinese full-scale missile attack.

Tips

  • Hsiung Feng IIE and HIMARS batteries can move into an assembly area (yellow area) and wait 40 minutes for reloading missiles if running out of munitions.
  • Default ready time has been set at 90 minutes for aircraft.
  • Damaged runways will be automatically repaired in 4 hours.
  • The more Chinese forces landing in the specific areas, the more Victory Points you will lose.
  • You will lose a lot of VPs if you attack Chinese assets first before the Chinese missile attack.
  • There are two satellites US provides to fly over the Chinese coast at 0200 and 0520.
