The initial blockade phase:

  • Use a submarine equipped with MK48s to sink the Chinese CSG entering Bachi Channel in order to break theblockade of the eastern waters of Taiwan.
  • Conduct an ASW mission in the northeastern waters of Taiwan.

ROE in this phase:

  • Any attacks on Chinese assets are not allowed.
  • Only submarines with MK-48s are allowed to attack Chinese surface ships or submarines, or ASW assets are allowed to attack Chinese submarines.

(Victory Points will be subtracted if breaking the ROE.)

The Chinese air and land strike phase:

  • Disperse some air assets from airbases to contingency runways.
  • Break the kill chain of the Chinese ballistic missile strike by destroying UAVs such as WZ-8s or BZK-005s.
  • Find and destroy MLRS launchers on Pingtang Island.
  • Destroy helicopters such as Z-10s at army air bases.
  • Conserve SAMs and anti-ship missile launchers as much as possible to counter the subsequent Chinese amphibious landing.
  • Attempt to attack SAMs along the Chinese coast for further cruise missile attacks against enemy’s air assets at airfields.
  • Ground-launched cruise missile launchers can move into the assembly area and wait 40 minutes for reloading missiles if running out of munitions.

The Chinese amphibious landing phase:

  • Execute an anti-ship missile strike against Chinese amphibious ships.
  • Use LT-2000, HIMARS and AH-64 to destroy amphibious vehicles.
  • Use SAM and SHORAD systems to deal with air assault.
