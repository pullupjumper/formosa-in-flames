Date/Time:9th June, 2027 / 01:00:00 Zulu

Location:Taiwan Strait

Duration:1 Day

Playable Sides:Taiwan


In the Spring of 2027, China is experiencing an economic crisis, leading Xi Jinping to use Taiwan issues to divert attention from the domemic crisis. China issued an ultimatum to Taiwan for starting negotiations for unification immediately. Meanwhile, it moved SAGs around Taiwan to prepare to establish a blockade of the island. Taiwan and the US intelligence agencies both obtained information that China had been planning a large-scale amphibious invasion of Taiwan on June 9 at 0400. Both had exhausted diplomatic means to try to dissuade Xi Jinping from the invasion, but to no avail. Nevertheless, the US government informed the Taiwanese government that it would not directly intervene in the conflict with China. The Taiwanese government was disappointed by this. Consequently, it had to consider confronting China militarily without US intervention. Therefore, Taiwan’s defense ministry proposed a preemptive attack against Chinese Carrier Strike Group in order to break the blockade of the eastern waters of Taiwan. The Taiwanese president agreed to the plan and subsequently announced the implementation of a joint sea-air anti-submarine exercise in the northeastern waters of Taiwan.
