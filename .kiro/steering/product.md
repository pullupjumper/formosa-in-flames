# Product Overview

This is a military simulation system focused on fire support planning and execution for wargaming scenarios. The system models complex military operations including:

- **Fire Support Planning (FSP)**: Dynamic creation and execution of fire support missions with multiple weapon systems (MLRS, SRBM, MRBM, GLCM)
- **Battle Damage Assessment (BDA)**: Automated evaluation of strike effectiveness and target damage
- **Reconnaissance Integration**: Satellite and UAV reconnaissance data processing for targeting decisions  
- **Air Tasking Orders (ATO)**: Coordination of air strikes with ground-based fire support
- **Electronic Warfare**: GPS jamming, communications jamming, and SIGINT operations
- **Integrated Air Defense Systems (IADS)**: Command and control modeling

The system appears to simulate cross-strait military scenarios with detailed modeling of Chinese PLA forces conducting operations against Taiwan-based targets. It includes realistic weapon systems, military units, and tactical procedures.

Key capabilities include dynamic target assessment, automated fire mission planning, multi-domain coordination, and real-time battle damage evaluation.
